## Menu

### Appetizers

- **Bruschetta Classica** ($12): Toasted ciabatta with tomatoes, basil, and balsamic glaze.
- **Calamari Fritti** ($16): Crispy squid served with lemon aioli.
- **Caprese Salad** ($14): Buffalo mozzarella, heirloom tomatoes, and fresh basil.

### Main Courses

- **Margherita Pizza** ($18): Classic tomato sauce, mozzarella, and basil.
- **Linguine alle Vongole** ($24): Linguine with fresh clams, garlic, and white wine sauce.
- **Grilled Sea Bass** ($32): Served with Mediterranean herbs and seasonal vegetables.
- **Osso Buco** ($36): Braised veal shanks with gremolata and risotto.

### Desserts

- **Tiramisu** ($10): Classic Italian coffee-flavored dessert.
- **Panna Cotta** ($9): Vanilla bean custard with berry coulis.

## Booking Policies

- **Reservations**: Recommended for parties of 6 or more.
- **Special Occasions**: We offer special arrangements for birthdays, anniversaries, etc.
- **Cancellations**: Please notify us at least 2 hours in advance.

## Accessibility & Parking

- **Parking**: Valet parking available; street parking also nearby.
